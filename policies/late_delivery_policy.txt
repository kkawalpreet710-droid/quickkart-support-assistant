QUICKKART LATE DELIVERY POLICY
Version 2.7 | Effective 1 September 2026
(Fictional policy written for a RAG learning project. QuickKart is not a real company.)

SECTION 1: DELIVERY ESTIMATE
1.1 When you check out, the app shows an estimated delivery time, usually between 10 and 20 minutes. Lateness is always measured against this estimate.
1.2 The estimate shown at checkout is locked when you place the order. If the live tracking time changes later, the checkout estimate is still the benchmark.

SECTION 2: COMPENSATION FOR LATE ORDERS
2.1 Grace period: if your order arrives up to 15 minutes after the estimate, no compensation is paid.
2.2 Delay of more than 15 minutes and up to 30 minutes: you receive Rs. 30 in QuickKart Wallet credit.
2.3 Delay of more than 30 minutes and up to 60 minutes: you receive Rs. 75 in QuickKart Wallet credit.
2.4 Delay of more than 60 minutes: you may cancel the order for a full refund, including the delivery fee and the handling fee. If you choose to keep the order and it is delivered, you receive Rs. 75 in QuickKart Wallet credit.
2.5 Late-delivery credits are added automatically within 30 minutes after delivery. You do not need to ask for them.
2.6 Late-delivery credits are wallet credit, not cash. They expire after 90 days, as described in the Payments and Wallet document.

SECTION 3: WHEN LATE COMPENSATION DOES NOT APPLY
3.1 Weather Delay Mode: when heavy rain, flooding, extreme heat, or a strike or bandh affects deliveries, the app shows a "Weather Delay Mode" banner at checkout. In this mode the estimate is not guaranteed and no late-delivery compensation is paid.
3.2 Customer-caused delays: no compensation is paid if the delay was caused by an incorrect or incomplete address, an unreachable phone number, or the delivery partner being unable to enter a gated building.

SECTION 4: FAILED DELIVERY ATTEMPTS
4.1 The delivery partner calls you twice and waits up to 5 minutes at your address.
4.2 If you cannot be reached, the order is taken back to the store and marked as undelivered.
4.3 For an undelivered order caused by the customer, perishable items are not refunded. If the order contains only non-perishable items, you receive a refund minus the delivery fee and the handling fee.

SECTION 5: SCHEDULED ORDERS
5.1 Scheduled orders are measured against the end of the delivery slot you chose, not against a checkout estimate. See Section 2 of the Delivery Fees and Slots document.
