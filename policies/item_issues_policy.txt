QUICKKART ITEM ISSUES POLICY
Version 1.9 | Effective 1 September 2026
(Fictional policy written for a RAG learning project. QuickKart is not a real company.)

SECTION 1: TYPES OF ITEM ISSUES
1.1 Missing item: an item you ordered and paid for is not in the delivery.
1.2 Wrong item: you received a different product, variant or size from the one you ordered.
1.3 Damaged item: the item or its packaging is broken, crushed or leaking.
1.4 Spoiled or poor-quality item: for example rotten fruit, sour milk or wilted vegetables.
1.5 Expired item: an item delivered after the expiry date printed on its label. You receive a full refund plus a Rs. 50 QuickKart Wallet credit.

SECTION 2: REPORTING WINDOWS
2.1 Perishable items: report within 24 hours of delivery. Perishable means fruits, vegetables, dairy, bakery, meat, seafood and frozen food.
2.2 Non-perishable items: report within 48 hours of delivery.
2.3 The window is counted from the delivery time shown in the app, not from when you opened the package.
2.4 Reports made after the window are reviewed only at QuickKart's discretion and may be declined.

SECTION 3: EVIDENCE REQUIRED
3.1 Missing items: no photo is needed. QuickKart checks the packing record instead.
3.2 Wrong, damaged and spoiled items: one clear photo of the item and its packaging or label is required.
3.3 Expired items: send a photo that clearly shows the expiry date on the label.
3.4 If a photo is unclear, support may ask for one more photo within 12 hours of your report.

SECTION 4: RESOLUTION OPTIONS
4.1 The default resolution is a refund, as described in the Refund Policy.
4.2 Replacement is available only for non-perishable items, only if the item is in stock at your nearest store, and only if you ask within the 48-hour window. A replacement is delivered with no delivery fee, usually within 2 hours.
4.3 You cannot receive both a refund and a replacement for the same item.
4.4 Perishable items are never replaced. They are refunded only.
