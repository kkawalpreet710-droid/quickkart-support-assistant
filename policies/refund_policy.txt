QUICKKART REFUND POLICY
Version 3.2 | Effective 1 September 2026
(Fictional policy written for a RAG learning project. QuickKart is not a real company.)

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW
1.1 A refund is money returned to you for something you paid for but did not receive in good condition. A refund is different from a compensation credit, which is a goodwill amount added to your QuickKart Wallet for a late delivery. Compensation credits are covered in the Late Delivery Policy.
1.2 You can get a refund when an item is missing, wrong, damaged, spoiled or expired, when an online-paid order is cancelled before delivery, or when QuickKart cancels your order.
1.3 To ask for a refund, open the app, go to Help > Orders, select the order, and tap Report an issue.

SECTION 2: REFUND AMOUNTS
2.1 Missing item: you receive a full refund of the price of the missing item, including the tax charged on that item.
2.2 Wrong item: you receive a full refund for the wrong item. Perishable wrong items do not need to be returned. For non-perishable wrong items, a pickup is arranged within 48 hours and the refund is processed after the pickup is completed.
2.3 Damaged, spoiled or expired item: you receive a full refund of the price of that item. A photo is required, as explained in the Item Issues Policy.
2.4 Partial refunds: only the affected items are refunded. The delivery fee and the handling fee are refunded only when the entire order is affected, for example when the whole order is missing or is cancelled by QuickKart.
2.5 Promo discounts: if a promo code discount was applied to your order, a refunded item is refunded at the amount you actually paid for it after the discount, not at its original listed price.

SECTION 3: REFUND TIMELINES AND DESTINATION
3.1 By default, refunds go back to the payment method used for the order.
3.2 Refund timelines by payment method are:
- QuickKart Wallet: instant, within 5 minutes
- UPI: 2 to 3 working days
- Credit or debit card: 5 to 7 working days
- Net banking: 5 to 7 working days
- Cash on delivery (COD): credited to your QuickKart Wallet within 24 hours, because no money was paid online
3.3 Instant wallet option: for any order paid online, you may choose to receive the refund as QuickKart Wallet credit instead of the original method. The credit arrives within 5 minutes. This choice cannot be reversed once it is made.
3.4 Working days means Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.
3.5 If your refund has not arrived after the timeline in clause 3.2, contact support in the app with your order ID. The case is escalated to the payments team, which replies within 48 hours.

SECTION 4: RETURNS AND NON-REFUNDABLE SITUATIONS
4.1 Change of mind: perishable products (fruits, vegetables, dairy, bakery, meat and frozen food) cannot be returned or refunded because you changed your mind. Sealed, unused non-perishable products in original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery. A return pickup fee of Rs. 30 is deducted from the refund.
4.2 Issues reported after the reporting window in the Item Issues Policy are not eligible for an automatic refund.
4.3 Orders that could not be delivered because of the customer (for example an unreachable phone) are handled under Section 4 of the Late Delivery Policy.
